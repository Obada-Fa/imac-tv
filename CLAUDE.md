# Nexus TV (formerly iMac TV): notes for Claude sessions

The name the user sees is Nexus TV (window title, wordmark, phone page, installers). Internal names stay `imactv` (data dir, `IMACTV_*` env vars, services, `~/tvremote`).

General orchestrator rules live in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` ("Orchestrator mode").

## Project facts
- **Phone app (2026-11-08):** `mobile/` is the Nexus TV Android app (Flutter, `com.nexus.tv`), moved here from the nexusRemote repo; Nexus Remote no longer has a TV page. See `mobile/README.md`. Build with `scripts/build-phone-app.sh`; test with `cd mobile && flutter test`.
- **Runtime:** the GTK3 10-foot TV app (`tvapp/home.py` shell, `tvapp/homescreen.py` ambient Home, `tvapp/widgets/`, `tvapp/screens.py` Browse/Search/Details), the phone web remote (`tvapp/server.py`, `tvapp/web/`), and media and playback (`tvapp/media/`, mpv, stremio-service).
- **Target TV:** a friend's Zorin laptop (Skylake i5 with Intel HD 520, Mesa GL, X11; 8 GB, 4 Docker containers). Claude has no access to it; the user tests there with `cd ~/tvremote && git pull && ./install.sh`.
- **CPU budget:** GTK3/cairo is software-rendered. Stay at about 0–2% of one core when calm and about 10% while animating, with ≤15 fps and dirty-region redraws only. Rasterize into a client-side ImageSurface and upload once per frame (`PhotoCanvas.on_draw` framebuffer): many strokes on the X surface cost the TV's Xorg ~60% of a core. Measure with `scripts/viz-bench.sh tvapp 30` (Xvfb is only a rough stand-in for the TV's Xorg). GtkGLArea was measured at 40–50% CPU (it reads frames back through cairo), so don't use it; the GPU visualizer draws into its own native X child window instead (`gl_ambient.py`).
- **Tests:** `cd tvapp && xvfb-run -a python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -q`. Never run them with DISPLAY=`:0`.
- **Running the server in tests:** set `IMACTV_DISPLAY` (private Xvfb), `IMACTV_DATA_DIR` (scratch dir), and `IMACTV_MPV_SOCK` for smoke runs.
- **Test-only hooks:** `IMACTV_FAKE_NOWPLAYING`, `IMACTV_FAKE_LEVELS`, `IMACTV_VIZ_MODES`, `IMACTV_GPU_VIZ`.
- **Settings files:**
  - `~/.local/share/imactv/home.json`: style, node_shape, tile_shape, shapes, visualizer, qr_style, and ambient interval/idle/idle_music.
  - `~/.local/share/imactv/media.json`: autoplay_next, auto_skip_intro, still_watching_*.

## Current state (2026-11-07)
- **Ambient Home:** layout as before the 2026-11-03 redesign (floating Continue watching tiles, top-right status chip, apps row ends with All apps, Search, Settings; no Search/System buttons anywhere). The clock (`widgets/clock.py` `NexusClock`) has five styles, picked in Settings → Appearance → Clock (home.json `clock_style`): glass (default: the date in a frosted pill like the status chip, the time large beneath it, a light running round the pill once a minute as the seconds), constellation (each digit a small star chart: stars of different sizes joined by thin lines that stop short of them, steady colon, a few faint background stars), analog (round frosted dial with hour bars, minute ticks and a glow seconds hand, the day beside it), simple (large light digits, unchanged), drift (handwritten digits of 44 motes each that regroup on the minute, date line under them). Glass, analog and drift redraw once a second while mapped, and Glass and analog only their pill or dial; simple and constellation redraw once a minute; the clock shrinks without the date in ambient (Glass keeps its pill). Not verified on the real TV. A waiting TV update is `island.UpdateIsland` on Home's top edge: it stays, the remote reaches it (up from the top row) and OK installs; it steps aside for notices and the volume island. Not verified on the real TV.
  - changing Bing/Wikimedia photo, clock, weather
  - network style (default): hexagon nodes and frosted tiles; bubbles is the alternative style
  - glass QR drop-down when no phone is connected
  - YouTube Now playing node
  - ambient mode after 120 s, or 30 s while YouTube plays: drifting particles and sky-line trace, weather line, and a music visualizer led by the edge-flow style
- **GPU visualizer (2026-11-07):** with hardware GL the ambient music visualizer is drawn at the display's refresh rate by `tvapp/gl_ambient.py`, a separate process (ctypes GLX in `glx.py`, no PyOpenGL) drawing into a native X child window of Home's toplevel: photo + music dim + the visualizer, interpolating between Home's frames (Home steps `shapes.Viz` at ~30 Hz, `GL_SIM_MS`). What is drawn is one description, `widgets/vizpaint.py`, with a cairo painter (homescreen `CairoPainter`) and a GL painter; GTK widgets that stay up (clock, caption, islands) get X Shape holes and cairo paints the visualizer inside them at 10 fps (`PhotoCanvas.only`). It shows only once the photo is fully dimmed and hides on wake, music stop or another screen; if the renderer fails or dies, Home stays on cairo for the session. `gpu.py` decides: home.json `gpu_viz` auto (default: hardware GL found, probed in a child process at start, cached in `gpu.json`; llvmpipe/softpipe/swrast = no GPU) / on / off, in Settings → Ambient "Smooth visualizer (GPU)"; `IMACTV_GPU_VIZ=1/0` forces it. On Xvfb (llvmpipe, forced on): Home 4.9% of a core vs 4.0% for the 10 fps cairo path; the renderer's own Python work is ~0.4 ms a frame. `scripts/gl-check.sh` (run on the TV) prints the detection, draws 20 s with fake music and reports fps, CPU, Xorg and the GPU clock. The mouse pointer is hidden whenever Home is ambient. The renderer stops drawing 0.25 s after Home stops sending frames (`STALE_S`) and never draws faster than 60 fps; Home also hides it whenever another app comes in front (before 2026-11-08 it could keep drawing full screen behind YouTube/AnyDesk: the user saw the TV lag). Not verified on the real TV.
- **Calm Home holds still (2026-11-07):** the tiles drift for `SETTLE_S` (6 s) after input, then the 20 fps timer stops (idle Home ~14% → ~1% of a core on Xvfb); a playing tile, fades, focus moves and the QR drop-down wake it. `/api/state` answers are shared for 1 s between the TV and phones (`Controller.shared_status`, any POST invalidates), wmctrl runs once per answer and xdpyinfo every 5 s. Not verified on the real TV.
- **YouTube decodes only what's seen (2026-11-07):** VacuumTube starts with `--enable-blink-features=AudioVideoTracks` (also in its flags.txt); while it plays behind Home, `youtube_volume.Link.set_video(False)` unselects the video track so Chromium stops decoding the picture (sound plays on); a YouTube started before the flag gets 144p instead. Pages also report VP9/AV1 as unsupported, so YouTube streams H.264. Checked in headless Chrome (MSE: 0 frames decoded while off); not verified in real VacuumTube or on the TV. Ambient's particles drift at 5 fps (`FIELD_MS`). The visualizer runs at 10 fps (`VIZ_MS`) and faded-out Home widgets are unmapped in ambient (`set_child_visible`): edge-flow 11.8% → 5.3% of a core on Xvfb.
- **Music casting (2026-11-07):** `tvapp/music_cast/receiver.mjs` (Node, `yt-cast-receiver` 2.1.0 pinned in package-lock) is "Nexus TV" in the YouTube / YouTube Music phone apps' cast menu (DIAL on port 8766, SSDP). Each song plays sound only in its own mpv (`--no-video`, yt-dlp `bestaudio`), Premium or not; the phone stays the remote and its volume is the system volume. `tvapp/music_cast.py` keeps it running from the phone server (restarts, cleans up leftover receiver/mpv), Home shows the song in the Now playing tile ("MUSIC · PLAYING", OK plays/pauses) and the visualizer follows it; a movie pauses it. `scripts/install-music-cast.sh` (from install.sh, no sudo) brings Node 20.10+ (Zorin's 18.19 can't load youtubei.js, so it downloads Node 22 to ~/.local/share/imactv/node, which is preferred) and checks the receiver loads, the official yt-dlp (~/.local/share/imactv/bin) and `npm ci`. Confirmed on the real TV (2026-11-07): casting from the YouTube Music app works; mpv audio ~3.5% CPU vs VacuumTube's ~48%, receiver ~0.5% idle, load average 10 → 3.2.
- **Phone app (2026-11-08):** saved TVs; a "Can't reach <TV>" page with Try again, saved TVs, TVs found on the Wi-Fi (`/api/hello` on port 8765 across the phone's /24), Scan QR (the app's own camera scanner, `mobile_scanner`, asks for camera permission; the Play services scanner failed on the user's phone) and Enter address (Tailscale 100.x works; the code is in Settings → Pair phone, which also lists the Tailscale address, or from `nexus-tv-pair` on the PC; `/api/pair-status` gives the code only to loopback and paired phones). An unpaired phone on the pairing page POSTs `/api/pair-request` (unauthenticated; 20 s per phone, 3 s overall): the server writes a `pair` ui_request with the code and Home opens the Pair phone sheet, or over another app shows "Pairing code" as a 30 s notice; the page has "Show the code on the TV again"; "Switch TV" in the remote opens it. The TV's QR (`/?pair=CODE`) answers Android browsers with `web/open-app.html`, which opens the app through `nexustv://pair?host=…&code=…` (intent link, browser fallback `&web=1`; the app's WebView loads `&app=1`). Links only open private addresses. Unit and widget tests pass; not verified on a real phone (no emulator image here).
- **Air pointer (2026-11-08, phone app only):** in Pointer mode the app adds "Hold to aim" to the touchpad (`mobile/assets/tv-native.js`): hold and tilt the phone to move the TV's mouse pointer, a quick tap (<220 ms) clicks. All the work is on the phone (`mobile/lib/air_pointer.dart`, `sensors_plus`): which way is up from the accelerometer (works flat or upright), tremor dead zone, smoothing, speed curve, whole pixels, ≤60 Hz. It streams 5-byte records (`<Bhh`: 0 move, 1 begin, 2 end, 3 click, 4 right click) over one chunked POST to `/api/air` on a raw socket (TCP_NODELAY, closed after 30 s idle); `Handler.phone_air` only unpacks them into XTest (`Controller.air_begin` focuses the app in front at most every 2 s). ~0.4% of a core on the TV at 60 Hz (Xvfb). Not on the web page: Chrome only gives motion sensors to HTTPS pages. Not verified on a real phone or the real TV (gains may need tuning: `AirMath` slowGain/fastGain/deadZone).

- **Arabic sources (2026-11-08):** `media/arabic.py` searches ArabP2P (a private tracker, login in `$DATA/arabic.json` 0600, set in TV Settings → Connections → Arabic sources or the phone's Settings) only when a title's sources are asked for (`addons.streams`, in a side thread) or a search is typed in Arabic (`addons.search` → Wikidata, or TMDB if `tmdb_key` is set → Cinemeta); answers are cached in `$DATA/cache/arabic`, nothing runs in the background. Titles are matched by their Arabic names (name must lead the release title; films need their own subtitle words; a year in the title must fit the season's air years from Cinemeta; anime counted across seasons match through `addons.running_number`). Labels come from ArabP2P categories: Arabic dub (71/98/100/102), Arabic subs (99/101/103), Arabic (originals). They lead the Sources list (dubs first) but `streams.best` only auto-plays them with Settings → Playback "Prefer Arabic" or after an Arabic episode, or when nothing else exists. Playing one: `media/torbox.py` downloads the .torrent with the session, adds it to TorBox (seed=auto, TorBox seeds private torrents back), polls until ready (stage "TorBox is downloading… N%"), picks the episode's file from a pack, and plays the link; never through the TV's own torrent engine (ratio). Packs over 80 GB are skipped. `scripts/arabic-check.py` signs in and prints what the parser sees. The main name also searches the dubbed categories (series 100/102, films 98) since a page holds ~30 releases. mpv gets `--alang=ar…` for dubs/Arabic and Arabic subtitles first for "Arabic subtitles" releases (`player.source_langs`). Confirmed on the real TV (2026-11-08): sign-in, TorBox fetch and playback of a Conan season pack (an Arabic-subtitles release; the dubbed one wasn't offered before the category search).
- **Twitch (ad-free):** Apps-row tile + `TwitchScreen` rail (top live streams) and a phone Search-tab block; `twitch.py` lists via public GQL and launches streamlink→mpv through `Controller.play_twitch`. Not verified on the real TV (needs streamlink; real Twitch ad filtering untested).
- **Brave as a TV app:** `tvapp/browser.py` launches a natively installed Brave in kiosk mode on its own profile (`~/.local/share/imactv/brave-tv`; logins and extensions need setting up there once) with a local debugging port. `widgets/browser_menu.py` is the TV menu. - **YouTube behind Home:** the window is minimized so the video stops rendering; audio is auto-resumed if minimizing paused it.
- **YouTube volume is the system volume:** `tvapp/youtube_volume.py` keeps the video element at full level over a local debugging port (9334). A lasting DevTools connection (`Link`) wraps VacuumTube's command hook: volume and mute from the phone's YouTube app set the system volume and show the TV island, and the TV's own volume and mute go back into VacuumTube, so the phone shows the same level. Checked in headless Chrome against a stand-in page; not verified in real VacuumTube or with the real phone app.
- **Volume on the TV:** the server writes `volume_event.json`; Home watches it and shows `widgets/volume_hud.py`: a frosted island on the top screen edge over Home and Settings, a hairline under the clock in ambient. Over other apps `island.IslandOverApps` draws each island frame off screen and hands it to the app in front (`tvapp/overlays.py`): YouTube (VacuumTube, port 9334) and the TV browser (Brave, port 9333) show it in their own page over DevTools, frosted live by the page's `backdrop-filter`; mpv (movies, series, Twitch) lays it over its video with `overlay-add` (solid glass); any other app, or one that doesn't answer, gets solid glass in its own `IslandWindow` (Zorin's GNOME paints a separate window's see-through part black). No picture of the app is copied while the island shows, so the old stale-bar glitch over video is gone. Confirmed by the user on the real TV over YouTube and over series (mpv). Not verified on the real TV.
- **Notices are islands:** `shell.toast()` shows `island.NoticeIsland` at the top edge (bead drops out, widens to fit the text, one light sweep, folds back), with icons per kind (phone, play, warning, done/update, info). It shows over apps too, steps aside while the volume island shows, and replaces the old bottom `Toast` and the server's `notify-send` update banner. Not verified on the real TV.
