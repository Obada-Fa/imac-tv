Circuit: 64 points, 56 short links, eight cached glow stamps, at most 24 cached
polylines with 96 vertices each. Constant bounded history; arc-length interpolation
uses binary search. No pixel access, edge detection, or pairwise neighbour search
in animation. Reuses the existing framebuffer, batched strokes and dirty cells.

Image detection runs once per uncached displayed crop on the photo worker.
The three reference images took about 0.5 seconds each at 384x216 in the initial
extraction experiment. Cached reloads do not decode the image for path detection.
Original background resolution is preserved by the existing canvas; the small
analysis copy and reduced-size GIF previews never replace the displayed image.

Synthetic tests cover colour-only boundaries, wall/floor bends, curved contours,
noise rejection, precise path attachment through kicks and reflections, photo
changes, cache invalidation, invalid data, and bounded frame cost.
